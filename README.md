<h1 align="center">JaZ Do It</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Show up. Do the work. We'll track the rest.</b></p>

Official Android downloads for **JaZ Do It**, a fitness app for the gym and the outdoors: guided workouts with every rep logged, GPS tracking for runs, walks, rides and hikes, and a 3D body map that shows which muscles are still recovering.

## Download

Get the latest APK from **[Releases](../../releases/latest)**.

1. Download `jaz-do-it-vX.Y.Z.apk` on your Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer).
2. Open it and allow **Install unknown apps** when asked.
3. Already installed? The app checks this page and offers new versions automatically.
